Getting Started

To create a league, click the "Create League" button on your dashboard. You'll be prompted to enter a league name and will receive a unique invite code to share with friends.

Click "Join League" on your dashboard and enter the invite code provided by your league commissioner. You'll be added to the league immediately.

TradeGrade supports leagues with 2-16 members. The optimal size is 8-12 members for the best fantasy experience.

Roster Management

Use the search function on your roster page to find players. Type any part of their name, team, or position. Click "Add Player" to add them to your roster.

Click the "Start" button next to any player on your bench to move them to your starting lineup. Click "Bench" to move them back to your bench.

Trade Analysis

Our AI analyzes player statistics, recent performance, expert consensus, and news to provide comprehensive trade recommendations. The analysis considers your team's needs and the impact on your roster.

We analyze player stats, injury reports, team matchups, expert rankings, recent news, and your team's positional needs to give you the most accurate trade recommendations.

Our AI combines multiple data sources and expert analysis to provide recommendations. While no system is 100% accurate, our analysis gives you valuable insights to make informed decisions.

Sit/Start Analysis

Click "Sit/Start?" next to any player to get AI-powered analysis considering their matchup, recent performance, injury status, and your team's alternatives.

If you only have one player at a position, the analysis will recommend starting them and suggest looking for alternatives on the waiver wire or through trades.
